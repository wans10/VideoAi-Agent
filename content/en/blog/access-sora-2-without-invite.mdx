## What Sora 2 Can Do Now\

Sora 2 isn’t just another text-to-video generator. It’s a **simulation engine** trained to model physics, lighting, and motion at near-photorealistic accuracy.\

### ✦ Key capabilities (from OpenAI’s official release)
- **Longer videos:** up to 15 seconds with temporal coherence across scenes.\

- **Multi-prompt sequencing:** creators can describe scene 1 → scene 2 → scene 3 in one structured timeline.\
- **Image-conditioning:** upload a still (like a logo or storyboard frame) to influence composition.\

## Why It’s Still Invite-Only\

According to OpenAI’s [system card](https://openai.com/index/sora-2-system-card), Sora 2’s rollout is phased. Access is limited to:
- verified researchers and safety reviewers,\
- select partners under usage agreements,\
- and a small subset of ChatGPT Pro users in the U.S. and Canada.\

OpenAI’s rationale: safety. The model can produce human likenesses, so geographic throttling and invite codes remain active until detection systems mature.\

For creators in Europe or Asia, the only legitimate way to experiment is through **authorized API distributors** like Fal.ai — and that’s precisely where VideoAI comes in.\

## Prompting Tips for Cinematic Results\

### 1️⃣ Structure your prompt like a storyboard\
Break it into time-coded scenes:\
```text
Scene 1 (0-3 s): aerial cityscape at dawn, warm light, soft piano.\
Scene 2 (3-8 s): close-up of character looking toward sunrise, gentle camera pan.\
Scene 3 (8-15 s): brand logo emerges in sky as music swells.\
```

### 2️⃣ Reference visual styles\

Use filmic keywords like anamorphic lens, 35 mm grain, cinematic lighting, depth of field.\

### 3️⃣ Add audio cues in the text\

“Soft ambient soundtrack with a rising bass hit at 5 seconds.”\

### 4️⃣ Use image inputs for brand consistency\

Drag your logo (.png) into VideoAI’s composer to embed it in-scene.\
